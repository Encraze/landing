Privacy Policy

Gengo · Effective date: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Gengo ("Gengo", "we", "us", "our") collects when you use the Gengo mobile application (the "App"), why we collect it, and the choices you have. We built Gengo to run on as little personal data as possible: the App's core job is to help you study Japanese, not to profile you.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

To create a Gengo account you sign in with one of: email and password, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple. Depending on which you choose, we store:

  • Your email address and a username you choose.
  • If you sign up with email and password, a one-way cryptographic hash of your password — we never store or have access to your actual password.
  • If you sign in with Google or Apple, an opaque account identifier from that provider used to recognize you on future sign-ins. We do not receive your Google or Apple password, and we do not request access to your contacts, photos, or other account data from Google or Apple.

App preferences

Settings you choose inside the App — timezone, light/dark theme, lesson pace, daily new-item limits, and whether you want reminder notifications.

Learning and usage data

The data the App needs to track your learning progress: your progress on each kanji, word, and radical; the results and timing of your quiz/review answers; your initial placement-test results; any notes or mnemonics you save; and how many lessons you've completed each day. This is used only to run spaced-repetition scheduling and show you your own progress.

Subscription information

If you buy a subscription, it is purchased through and processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store — Gengo never sees or stores your payment card details. We store only a subscription/purchase token and status (e.g. active, canceled, expired) so the App can verify what you're entitled to.

Device and notification data

If you allow notifications, we store a push-notification token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) linked to your account, used solely to deliver reminders you asked for. You can disable this at any time in the App's settings.

Data stored only on your device

The App also keeps a local, on-device copy of your progress and settings so it keeps working offline. That local copy stays on your device — it is not something we collect, and it is removed if you uninstall the App.

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and secure your account, and keep you signed in.
  • Run the App's core learning features: scheduling reviews, tracking progress, and syncing it across your devices.
  • Send reminder notifications you've opted into.
  • Verify and manage your subscription.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the App and its backend.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not run third-party analytics or ad SDKs inside the App, and we do not sell personal information to anyone.

4. Data Retention

We keep your account and learning data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (see below), your email, identities, saved progress, hints, and device tokens are deleted or anonymized immediately, and the remaining account record is permanently purged from our systems within 30 days.

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time from within the App: open Settings, choose Delete Account, and confirm using the one-time code we email you. Once confirmed, your learning progress, saved hints, linked sign-in identities, and notification tokens are deleted, and your account is deactivated.

If you'd rather not use the in-app flow, you can request deletion, access, or correction of your data by emailing us at gengo.admin@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal data — for example, under the EU/UK GDPR the rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and data portability, plus the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority; or under US state privacy laws (e.g. CCPA/CPRA) the rights to know what personal information we hold, request its deletion, and opt out of its sale — which we do not do, in any case.

6. Children's Privacy

Gengo is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Passwords are stored only as one-way cryptographic hashes, session and verification codes are stored hashed rather than in plain text, and data in transit between the App and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard practices.

8. International Data Transfer

Our backend and database run on Google Cloud infrastructure located in the EU. If you use the App from outside the EU, your information will be transferred to and processed in the EU.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the effective date above, and if a change is material we'll let you know inside the App.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at gengo.admin@gmail.com.
